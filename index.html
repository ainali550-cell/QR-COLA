OUR STORY

HOW WE STARTED THE RUSH.

It started in a garage in 2021 with a simple realization: modern sodas are boring. They lose their fizz too fast, they’re packed with junk, and the branding puts you to sleep.

We wanted a drink that hit different. Something engineered for maximum carbonation. Something that stays ice-cold and delivers a rush with every single sip. After 142 recipe iterations, QR Cola was born. And to make it even better, we connected the physical can to the digital world—giving back to our community with every scan.

Alex & Sarah Founders, QR Cola
GARAGE DAYS, 2021
